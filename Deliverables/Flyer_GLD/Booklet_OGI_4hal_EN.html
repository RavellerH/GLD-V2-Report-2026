THE PRINCIPLE

Invisible gas.
Made visible.

Carbon-hydrogen bonds absorb infrared radiation near 3.3 µm. A camera with a narrow spectral filter on that band renders the gas as contrast against the background.
MWIR optics3–5 µm band
Spectral filter~3.3 µm, C–H absorption band
Cooled detector≈ −196 °C (77 K)
3D illustration of a generic cooled MWIR camera.
MWIR band

Mid-Wave Infrared, 3–5 µm, where hydrocarbons absorb radiation.

Narrow filter

Passes only the C–H absorption wavelength.

Cryogenic detector

Cooling reduces detector noise, improving sensitivity to weak infrared gas signatures.
